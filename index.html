FAQ

Good questions

What can I bring in to be engraved?

Metal, powder-coated, wood and genuine leather items with a flat or gently curved face — tumblers (one side), flasks, knives, hand tools, lighters and tags — up to about 8.5 × 8.5 inches of engraving area. Most bring-your-own items are $20, or $15 each when you bring 5 or more. We can't engrave glass, ceramic mugs, clear acrylic, or PVC and vinyl plastics.

Will the engraving wear off?

No. It's burned into the material rather than printed on top, so it won't peel, fade or wash away.

Do I see a proof first?

Always. We send a digital preview showing size and placement, and nothing is engraved until you approve it.

How long does it take?

Most orders take 3–5 business days. Need it sooner? Ask and we'll do our best.

How do I pay?

Send your order request and we'll confirm the details and total. You can pay by e-transfer or when you pick up.

What file should I send for a logo?

Vector files (SVG, PDF, AI) are ideal. A high-resolution PNG works too, and we can clean up most logos for you.
