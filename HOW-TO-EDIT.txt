BACK LOT PRINTING — WEBSITE FILES
==================================

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html     Homepage
  shop.html      Shop (all products, personalize, add to order)
  order.html     Customer's order + "Send order request"
  config.js      YOUR DETAILS  <- edit this
  products.js    YOUR PRODUCTS <- edit this to add/remove/reprice items
  styles.css     Look and layout (no need to edit)
  site.js        How it works (no need to edit)

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — ADD YOUR DETAILS (config.js)
------------------------------------------------------------
Right-click config.js > Open with > Notepad (Mac: TextEdit).
Fill in the text inside the quotes:
  town, email, phone, instagram, facebook,
  turnaround, pickup
Keep the quotes " " and the commas. Save.

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — CHECK YOUR PRODUCTS (products.js)
------------------------------------------------------------
Open products.js the same way. Each line is one product.
  Change a price:  edit the number after  price:
  Remove an item:  delete its whole line
  Add an item:     copy a line, paste it, change the details
                   (give it a new id, like "d9")
Save.

------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — UPLOAD TO GITHUB
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open your GitHub repository.
2. Click "Add file" > "Upload files".
3. Drag in ALL SEVEN files from this folder.
   (The new index.html replaces your coming-soon page.
    Want to keep the coming-soon page live for now? Upload
    everything EXCEPT index.html until you're ready.)
4. Click "Commit changes". Your site updates in a minute or two.

------------------------------------------------------------
HOW ORDERS REACH YOU
------------------------------------------------------------
Customers add items, then press "Send order request".
  - By default this opens THEIR email app with the full order
    written out, addressed to the email in config.js.
  - To receive orders automatically instead, make a free form
    at formspree.io and paste its link into formEndpoint in
    config.js.
No money is taken online. You reply with a proof and total,
and they pay by e-transfer or at pickup.

------------------------------------------------------------
ADDING YOUR OWN PHOTOS (optional)
------------------------------------------------------------
Make a folder called  images  and put your photos in it.
In index.html, search for  "TO USE YOUR OWN PHOTO"  — the
note right there shows exactly what to swap in.
