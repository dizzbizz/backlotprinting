FAQ

Good questions about custom shirts, DTF printing and engraving

What is DTF shirt printing?

DTF (direct-to-film) prints your design in full colour onto a special film, then we heat-press it onto the garment. It works on cotton, polyester, blends, canvas and denim, on light or dark colours, so we can do shirts, hoodies, jackets, aprons, bags, pillow covers and more. There is no minimum: one piece is a real order. Wash inside out in cold water and it lasts.

Can you print any design on a shirt?

We print your own artwork, logos you own, names, numbers, photos and text. We can't print sports team logos, brands or characters you don't have the rights to. Send a high-resolution PNG (300 dpi at print size) or a vector file and we'll check it before printing.

How long do DTF prints last?

A DTF print is pressed into the fabric, not stuck on top like an iron-on, so it stretches with the shirt and holds its colour. Looked after properly, it should last as long as the garment does. Wash inside out in cold water, hang dry or tumble dry low, and skip the bleach.

Can I bring my own garment?

Yes. Bring your own tee, hoodie, jacket, apron or tote and we'll press your design on, from $15 for a front print. Cotton, polyester, blends, canvas and denim all work. If we're not sure a fabric will take the heat, we'll tell you before we press it.

Is there a setup fee?

No. There's no setup fee and no minimum order, and you get a proof before we print. If your artwork needs a lot of redrawing we'll let you know the cost first, before any work is done.

What can I bring in to be engraved?

Metal, powder-coated, wood and genuine leather items with a flat or gently curved face — tumblers, flasks, knives, hand tools, lighters and tags — up to about 8.5 × 8.5 inches of engraving area. Most bring-your-own items are $20 for one side, or $15 each when you bring 5 or more. Want a tumbler done on both sides? That's $30. We can't engrave glass, ceramic mugs, clear acrylic, or PVC and vinyl plastics.

Will the engraving wear off?

No. It's burned into the material rather than printed on top, so it won't peel, fade or wash away.

Do I see a proof first?

Always. We send a digital preview showing size and placement, and nothing is engraved until you approve it.

How long does it take?

Most orders take 1–5 business days. Need it sooner? Ask and we'll do our best.

What if I'm not happy with it?

If your finished piece doesn't match the proof you approved, we'll redo it on a new blank at no charge, or give you your money back. On items you bring in, we test a hidden spot first where we can, and if something goes wrong on our end we'll refund the engraving and work out a fair fix with you.

Where are you, and do you deliver?

We're a small shop in Sherwood Park, Alberta, with pickup by appointment. We regularly have customers from Strathcona County, Edmonton and Fort Saskatchewan. Shipping is available on most items, just ask.

How do I pay?

Send your order request and we'll confirm the details and total. You can pay by e-transfer or when you pick up.

Can you personalize 3D-printed toys?

Yes. We print our own toy designs in the colours you pick, then add a name, either printed right into the toy or laser-engraved afterwards. Perfect for birthdays, stocking stuffers and party favours. Toys have small parts and aren't meant for children under 3.

What file should I send for a logo?

Vector files (SVG, PDF, AI) are ideal. A high-resolution PNG works too, and we can clean up most logos for you.
